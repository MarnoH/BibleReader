Bybel-app

A Bible reader that turns its pages, for the phone and the computer

Bybel-app is a free Bible reader laid out like a printed Bible: pages that turn, a bookshelf of the books of the Bible, ribbons and a reading register. It is made for reading the whole Bible regularly, for study in the original languages, and for keeping notes as you read. It works in English and Afrikaans and, once opened, without a connection.

What it does

  • The Bible in the Afrikaans 1933/53 translation (AOV), the King James Version and the Berean Standard Bible, with the Hebrew Old Testament and four Greek New Testaments; two can be read side by side.
  • Study: tap a word for its Strong's number, meaning and parsing; cross-references; a page comparing the Greek texts of the New Testament.
  • Notes: highlights and coloured words, notes and sermon notes (typed or with a pen), questions to answer later, and a memory palace for learning Scripture by heart.
  • Reading register: every chapter read, with the date, to see how the whole Bible is being read.
  • Library: other good books to read alongside, from PDF, CCEL's ThML or Markdown, or written in the app itself; with bookmarks, notes and highlights.

Your data stays with you

Everything you write or mark is kept in the browser on your own device. There is no account, no server of ours, no advertising and no tracking. To move your notes to another device you can save a backup file, or, if you choose, connect your own Google Drive.

Google Drive (optional)

In the settings, Connect Google Drive lets you keep a copy of your notes, reading register and library in your own Google Drive, so your phone and computer stay in step. The app asks only for permission to the files it creates itself (a folder named “Bybel-app”); it cannot see anything else in your Drive. Your data goes straight between your device and Google, and never to us. See the privacy policy for the details.

’n Bybelleser wat sy bladsye omblaai, vir die foon en die rekenaar

Bybel-app is ’n gratis Bybelleser wat lyk soos ’n gedrukte Bybel: bladsye wat omblaai, ’n boekrak met die boeke van die Bybel, lintjies en ’n leesregister. Dit is gemaak om die hele Bybel gereeld te lees, vir studie in die grondtale, en om notas te hou terwyl jy lees. Dit werk in Afrikaans en Engels, en sodra dit oopgemaak is, ook sonder verbinding.

  • Die Bybel in die Ou Vertaling (AOV), die King James en die Berean Standard Bible, met die Hebreeuse Ou Testament en vier Griekse Nuwe Testamente; twee kan langs mekaar gelees word.
  • Studie: tik op ’n woord vir die Strong-nommer, betekenis en ontleding; kruisverwysings; ’n bladsy wat die Griekse tekste vergelyk.
  • Notas: merke en gekleurde woorde, notas en preeknotas (getik of met ’n pen), vrae om later te beantwoord, en ’n geheuepaleis om die Skrif uit die kop te leer.
  • Leesregister: elke hoofstuk wat gelees is, met die datum.
  • Biblioteek: ander goeie boeke om saam te lees, uit PDF, ThML of Markdown, of in die app self geskryf.
