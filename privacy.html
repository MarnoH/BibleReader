Privacy policy

Bybel-app · https://marnoh.github.io/BibleReader/ · last updated 24 October 2026

In short

Bybel-app keeps what you write and mark on your own device. It has no accounts, no server of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Only if you choose to connect Google Drive does the app store a copy of your data, and then only in your own Google Drive.

What the app keeps, and where

Your notes, sermon notes, highlights, questions, memory palaces, reading register, settings, and the books you add or write in the library are stored in your browser on your device (its local storage and IndexedDB). They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them; a backup file you save is yours to keep or delete.

Hosting

The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses when the files are fetched; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The app itself sets no cookies.

Google Drive (optional)

If you tap Connect Google Drive in the settings, Google's own sign-in asks you to allow the app access. The app requests only the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which gives it access only to the files it creates itself; it cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

  • What is stored: a folder named “Bybel-app” with up to two files: bybel-notas.json (your notes, sermon notes, highlights, memory palaces, questions and reading register) and biblioteek.json (your own library books, their notes, highlights and bookmarks, where you are in each book and your reading time).
  • How it is used: only to sync your data between your own devices: when you tap Sync, the app reads these files, merges them with the data on the device and writes the result back.
  • Where it goes: straight between your device and Google. The developer of Bybel-app never receives, sees or stores your Google data, and it is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone.
  • Sign-in: the access token Google gives is kept in the app's memory only while it is open, and is not stored or sent anywhere else. The app does not receive your password.
  • Stopping: tap Disconnect in the settings, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The files stay in your Drive until you delete the “Bybel-app” folder yourself.

Bybel-app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.

Privaatheidsbeleid

Bybel-app · laas bygewerk 24 Oktober 2026

Bybel-app hou wat jy skryf en merk op jou eie toestel. Daar is geen rekeninge, geen bediener van die app, geen advertensies, geen ontledings en geen dop hou nie. Jou notas, preeknotas, merke, vrae, geheuepaleise, leesregister, instellings en die boeke in jou biblioteek word in die blaaier op jou toestel gestoor en nie na ons of iemand anders gestuur nie.

Google Drive (opsioneel)

As jy Koppel Google Drive kies, vra die app net toegang tot die lêers wat dit self maak (drive.file): ’n vouer “Bybel-app” met bybel-notas.json en biblioteek.json. Dit word net gebruik om jou data tussen jou eie toestelle te sinkroniseer, gaan reguit tussen jou toestel en Google, en word nooit deur die ontwikkelaar ontvang, gesien of met iemand gedeel nie. Ontkoppel in die instellings of by myaccount.google.com/permissions; die lêers bly in jou Drive totdat jy die vouer self skrap. Die gebruik van inligting van Google API’s volg die Google API Services User Data Policy, insluitend die Limited Use-vereistes.
